## Equations and variables

For the approved vertical geometry,

\[
L=h_{\rm HAP}-h_{\rm UAV}=19\,000\ {\rm m}.
\]

The active frozen model uses the Kruse/Beer--Lambert power law and Gaussian
beam/aperture law

\[
q(V)=1.6\quad(V>50\ {\rm km}),\qquad
\xi=\frac{3.912}{V_{\rm km}}
\left(\frac{\lambda_{\rm nm}}{550}\right)^{-q}\ [{\rm km}^{-1}],
\]

\[
\eta_{\rm atm}=e^{-\xi L_{\rm km}},\quad
z_R=\frac{\pi W_0^2}{\lambda},\quad
W_L=W_0\sqrt{1+(L/z_R)^2},\quad
T_0^2=1-e^{-2a^2/W_L^2}.
\]

Receiver-plane variance follows the frozen equations

\[
\sigma_{\rm turb}^2=1.919 C_n^2L^3(2W_0)^{-1/3},
\]

\[
\sigma_{\rm UAV}^2=\sigma_x^2+\sigma_y^2+\sigma_z^2+
a^2(\sigma_\theta^2+\sigma_\phi^2+\sigma_\psi^2),
\qquad
\sigma_r^2=\sigma_{\rm turb}^2+\sigma_{\rm UAV}^2.
\]

Here `sigma_r` is the standard deviation of each Cartesian displacement axis
and therefore the Rayleigh scale of radial displacement `r`; radial RMS is
`sqrt(2) sigma_r`. With the Bessel-derived pointing parameters `Gamma` and
`R`, instantaneous power transmittance is

\[
T=\eta_{\rm atm}T_0^2\exp[-(r/R)^\Gamma],qquad
r\sim {\rm Rayleigh}(\sigma_r).
\]

Thus its exact physical support is

\[
0<T\le \eta_{\rm atm}T_0^2=0.0289196729404660.
\]

The lower endpoint is open; the on-axis upper endpoint is included.

## Joint-state Monte Carlo recipe

1. Use independent train, validation, and test base seeds already frozen in
   the experiment configuration. Derive separate BLAKE2b-namespaced streams
   for `T` and epsilon inside every split.
2. Draw independent zero-mean Cartesian displacement components whose common
   per-axis variance is `sigma_r^2`, equivalently draw
   `r ~ Rayleigh(sigma_r)`.
3. Transform `r` through the pointing law above and multiply by atmospheric
   power transmittance.
4. Independently draw input-referred
   `epsilon ~ Uniform[0.001,0.04]` SNU. This is an author-approved assumed
   operating-domain distribution, not an experimentally measured HAP--UAV
   joint distribution. No physical coupling is claimed.
5. Hash `T`, epsilon, and paired states and reject any split seed, realization,
   or exact-pair reuse. These are iid Monte Carlo states, not a temporally
   correlated UAV trajectory.

## Validity, sanity checks, and failure modes

- The 200 km visibility is explicitly a **nominal good-weather homogeneous
  Kruse sensitivity scenario**. It does not model cloud, fog, rain, or a
  vertically resolved extinction profile.
- `C_n^2=10^-16 m^-2/3` is treated as constant over 19 km. The active model
  includes its beam-wander term but no log-normal, Gamma--Gamma, Malaga,
  scintillation, turbulence-induced beam spread, or altitude-profile term.
  The plane-wave constant-`C_n^2` Rytov diagnostic is about `0.44`, within a
  weak-to-moderate diagnostic range, but it is not a validation of every
  omitted turbulence effect for a finite Gaussian beam.
- UAV position and attitude components are independent, zero-mean Gaussian;
  deterministic boresight is zero. A nonzero boresight would require a
  noncentral pointing law and must not reuse this Rayleigh model.
- Gaussian diffraction/aperture collection already provides geometric loss.
  No extra inverse-square factor may be multiplied into `T`.
- `r=0` must produce the upper support endpoint; increasing `r` must strictly
  reduce `T`. Infinite visibility gives `eta_atm -> 1`; zero jitter collapses
  the distribution to the on-axis value.
- Generation fails if any `T<=0`, `T>eta_atm T0^2`, epsilon is outside its
  nonnegative interval, either state coordinate has zero variance, inputs are
  nonfinite, or the approved independence metadata is absent.
- The model includes no additional transmitter/receiver optical efficiency.
  Adding such throughput is a separate author/model decision and cannot be
  hidden inside this diagnostic.

Recommended validation plots are the `T` histogram with analytic quantiles,
Rayleigh radial histogram/Q--Q plot, epsilon histogram, joint `(T,epsilon)`
scatter with correlation reported only as a finite-sample diagnostic, and
split-specific empirical CDFs/hashes.

## Exact downstream handoff

- QAM/PS/VA receives `[log10(T), epsilon]`, where `T` is instantaneous **power
  transmittance** and epsilon is input-referred SNU. GS remains global.
- CV-QKD receives the identical `T`, the identical input-referred epsilon, and
  the identical physical ensemble used by mutual information.
- Do not pass field amplitude `sqrt(T)`, received-power SNR, detector-output
  noise, radial displacement, or atmospheric/aperture factors separately in
  place of the composite `T`.
