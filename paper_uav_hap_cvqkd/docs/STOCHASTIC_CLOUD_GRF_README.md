# Spatiotemporal Stochastic Cloud Field Model using Spatially Correlated GRF, Wind Advection and AR(1) Temporal Evolution

Independent **synthetic cloud generation only**. No optical blockage, FSO
attenuation, CLWC conversion, QKD, SKR, finite-size or UAV optimization is
implemented. Existing Ibrahim producers, configurations and results are preserved.

## Spatial GRF: exact covariance at the required sample points

```text
Ks(r,r') = exp(-norm(r-r')/ell_c)
G0, eta1, eta2,... ~ independent N(0,Ks)
```

The FFT circulant embedding starts with an exponential first covariance row:

```text
c[i,j] = exp(-hypot(min(i,Ny-i)*dy_internal,
                   min(j,Nx-j)*dx_internal)/ell_c)
lambda = real(FFT(c))
G = inverseFFT(sqrt(lambda)*FFT(iid standard-normal W))
```

The usual forward/unscaled, inverse/1-N FFT convention gives covariance c
without any extra sample normalization. The final field is spatially correlated;
the white noise is only an input to a linear Gaussian filter. DC is retained.
Both the ensemble mean zero and marginal variance one are mathematical targets,
not enforced spatial moments of each finite crop. No sample-std divisor,
per-frame recentering, jitter or covariance renormalization is used.

The embedding is accepted only when all eigenvalues are nonnegative. Any
negative eigenvalue, even a small one, raises an error; none is clipped.
Imaginary residuals and reconstruction error are recorded. If another config
fails this gate, enlarge `guard_m`/`embedding_scale` and explicitly regenerate;
the code never silently substitutes a different covariance. The default
eigenvalue minimum is about 0.01249935, well away from zero.

All queried output-grid separations and validation space-time differences are
inside the embedding half-period in each coordinate. Its minimum-image first
row therefore equals the requested **unwrapped exponential covariance** at
those points. The exact target/embedding match is checked separately from Monte
Carlo error; positive spectrum alone would not establish that match.

For every pair of exported ROI points/times, the largest possible component of
h-v*m*dt is 1960+4995=6955 m in x and 1960 m in y, below the 8000/3000 m
half-periods. Thus the full exported discrete Gaussian space-time covariance,
not just the selected validation lags, agrees with the ideal kernel up to
floating-point error. `covariance_support_validation.json` records these bounds.

## Exact wind on an internal lattice

Repeated linear interpolation is avoided. A finite 40 m spectral interpolant
cannot in general equal an exponential covariance at arbitrary fractional
offsets, particularly near the cusp at zero. The default uses internal spacing
**5 m in x, 40 m in y**, and samples every eighth x node to export 40-by-40 m
output cells. Thus -5 m/slot is an **exact one-column internal permutation**,
equivalent to -0.125 output cell/slot and -1 output cell after eight slots.

This deliberate alternative to fractional Fourier advection retains the exact
requested covariance for every required x-offset h-v*m*dt: with the configured
wind/time step these are all multiples of 5 m. No band-limited subcell covariance
approximation is needed. The Gaussian innovations are still generated by FFT.
The advection operator is `np.roll` on the **large synthetic domain**, never
on the observation ROI. Frozen-field values and covariance are preserved to
permutation/roundoff accuracy. Frozen validation runs before evolving AR fields.

`refine_x`, `refine_y` must make both physical wind displacements integral on
the internal lattice. Incommensurate settings fail explicitly; the code does
not round physical motion or add a hidden interpolation. Increase refinement
when changing dt/wind. Temporal covariance at off-lattice points or between
time slots is not simulated. This internal refinement does not imply measured
5 m meteorological information. Output morphology remains resolved at 40 m.

## AR(1) law and space-time covariance

```text
rho = exp(-dt/tau_c)
alpha = sqrt(1-rho^2) = sqrt(-expm1(-2*dt/tau_c))
G[k+1](r) = rho*G[k](r-v*dt) + alpha*eta[k+1](r)
```

Advection A is a grid permutation. C is circulant and translation-invariant,
so `A C A^T=C`. Independent eta has the same covariance as G0. Starting with
G0~N(0,C), `Cov(G_next)=rho^2*C+alpha^2*C=C`; the Gaussian Markov law is
stationary. The factor alpha alone would not ensure stationarity for an
operator that changes covariance. Spatial correlation ell, wind v and intrinsic
decorrelation tau have separate roles.

For h measured **from the earlier point to the later point** and m>=0:

```text
G[k+m](r+h) = rho^m*G[k](r+h-v*m*dt) + independent future innovations
R(h,m) = E[G[k](r)*G[k+m](r+h)]
       = rho^m*exp(-norm(h-v*m*dt)/ell_c)
```

The minus sign follows directly from the pullback. At h=v*m*dt the coefficient
is rho^m. At fixed Eulerian h=0, wind reduces correlation further. For the
default m=8, the peak is h_x=-40 m, not +40 m. Validation evaluates both signs,
axial/diagonal spatial lags, fixed h at several temporal lags and material paths.
The material estimator is per-seed `sum(G0*Gm_material)/sum(G0^2)`, whose
conditional expectation is rho^m. Its finite estimates need not stay in [-1,1].

Population variance within a correlated ROI has expectation
`1-sum_{r,s in ROI} Ks(r-s)/M^2`, which is smaller than one. The runner computes
this value exactly for the 50-by-50 ROI; it tests this statistic separately
from `mean(G^2)`, whose target is one. Histogram checks are descriptive; pixels
are not treated as independent normality-test samples. Unit tests use separate
independent GRF draws for marginal-Gaussian tests.

## Padding and no premature re-entry

Default internal dimensions **3200 x 150**, extent **16000 x 6000 m**. Domain
construction includes two times the ROI width, total wind travel and guard,
then rounds upward to efficient FFT sizes. The crop's physical edges are
x=[1000,3000] m and y=[1020,3020] m in synthetic coordinates. Output coordinates
are reset to [0,2000] m, with centres 20,60,...,1980 m in both axes.

Conservative padding horizon: 1000 s (the last output is k=999, t=999 s).
Backward x-intervals are [1000,3000] at t0, [3000,5000] at t400,
[5995,7995] at t999 and [6000,8000] at t1000. Every innovation has age no
greater than the horizon, so its source remains between these bounds, without
modulo reduction. There is no periodic cloud re-entry in the observed mission.
A tagged seam impulse and an in-ROI positive control test the sampling gate.
The same bounds protect material probes; validated covariance offsets are
also explicitly compared with the unwrapped kernel.

The full synthetic process is finite and periodic. It is not an infinite-domain
weather model; arbitrary lags/durations outside the validated support could
see periodic images. Simulator updates beyond the configured horizon fail.

## Relative intensity and presence diagnostic

```text
I = A*max(0,G-theta_c)^q, with A>0 and q>0
diagnostic_presence = (G>theta_c)
```

Defaults A=q=1. Threshold modes:

- `ensemble`: theta=Phi^-1(0.70), using the known N(0,1) marginal law once.
  Expected initial fraction is 30%; a finite initial crop need not equal 30%.
- `initial`: theta is the empirical 70th percentile of that seed's initial ROI.
- `fixed`: supply `theta_c` directly.

Theta is fixed for the full mission, never recomputed per frame. The default
ensemble threshold is independent of the realization. I is a nonnegative
**uncalibrated relative cloud-field proxy**, not CLWC in mg/m^3; the diagnostic
presence mask is not optical blockage. I has an atom at zero and is non-Gaussian.
The latent covariance formula cannot be assigned to I or the mask. With a fixed
deterministic transform, their distributions inherit stationarity, but their
correlation must be measured or derived separately. Initial-quantile calibration
instead conditions the diagnostic on the initial realization.

## Morphology and observed segment lifetimes

Presence components are 8-connected on the output grid, with area = cell count
times 1600 m^2. ROI edges are not periodically joined. The diagnostic tracker
uses wind-weighted overlap at the coarse grid; bilinear **indicator association
weights** have no role in latent-field evolution. A pair continues only when
both components have a unique partner above the configured IoU threshold.
Ambiguous split/merge events terminate identities and start new segments.

`track_segments.csv` records first/last detection, observed span=(last-first)*dt,
start/end reason and ROI/time censoring. These are observed feature-segment
lifetimes, not physical cloud lifetimes or an unbiased survival distribution.
Threshold crossings and coarse sampling can generate many short segments.
Morphology does not enforce mass conservation, thermodynamics or a measured
cloud-size/lifetime distribution. Material-frame RMS change and presence IoU
measure shape evolution separately, using exact wind compensation.

## Validation, figures and provenance

`cloud_fields.npz`: all 1000 cropped latent/intensity/presence arrays,
coordinates, time and JSON parameters; load with `allow_pickle=False`.
`frozen_fields.npz`: paired frozen baseline from the same initial realization.
`ensemble_validation.npz`: independent-seed moments, histograms, spatial and
space-time covariance, material correlation. CSV files export all statistics
and track segments. Approximate pointwise Student-t 95% intervals use independent
seeds, not correlated pixels or slots. Four-SE descriptive checks and numerical
tolerances are configured before the ensemble run; failed checks are retained.

A-H are exported as PNG400dpi and PDF. F/G are vector curves; spatial images
are raster grids with vector labels/axes. GIF has all 1000 slots, y-up orientation,
fixed intensity palette and 50 ms accelerated playback. A contains marginal
histograms, D includes morphology and observed spans, H compares frozen/evolving
fields including material-frame MSE against 2(1-rho^k).

The pre-existing dirty worktree is recorded and preserved. Producer/config
hashes, original diff/handoff, protected old-input hashes, spectrum, boundary,
frozen test and result receipts accompany the package. Regression tests exercise
both old Ibrahim modules as well as the new one. Full repository suite and all
physical/QKD integration remain NOT_RUN. Publication/security states and the
historical frozen-spec hash discrepancy are unchanged.

## Relationship to literature

[Peleg et al. (2017)](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2016MS000854)
describes AWE-GEN-2d, combining stochastic/physical weather modules with
calibration data. [Carpentieri et al.'s author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.07293)
describes SolarSTEPS: scale-dependent AR(p), cloud-motion estimation and spatially
structured innovations (Sections 2.2-2.4, Eq.2). The specified published reference
is DOI [10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.121775](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.121775);
the accessible preprint has a different title wording/order of authors.

Our single exponential covariance, one constant rho, prescribed uniform wind,
5 m internal lattice and positive-part intensity are a **proposed simplified
synthetic model**, not a verbatim equation or reproduction of either weather/
forecast system. Source-access boundaries are recorded in `references.json`.
The difference from the existing Ibrahim-style model is the explicit isotropic
exponential covariance and exact commensurate advection, rather than a weighted
multiscale band-limited spectrum; no overall physical superiority is asserted.
