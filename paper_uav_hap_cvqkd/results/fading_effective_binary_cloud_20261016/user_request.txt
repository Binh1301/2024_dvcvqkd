## 3. Implement the Three Fading Moments

For each candidate UAV position j, evaluate:

\[
m_{1,j}=\langle T_j\rangle,
\]

\[
m_{1/2,j}=\langle\sqrt{T_j}\rangle,
\]

\[
m_{\xi,j}=\langle T_j\xi_j(T_j)\rangle.
\]

These correspond to the analytical fading moments in equations (19)–(21) of the manuscript.

Reuse the existing validated closed-form fading-moment functions wherever possible.

Do not introduce Monte Carlo approximations where verified analytical moments already exist.

Preserve the manuscript's integration interval [0,1], normalization convention and treatment of the probability mass at zero transmittance.

Explicitly distinguish AoA-induced zero-transmittance events from the new binary-cloud blockage states. Do not accidentally change conditional normalization or count outages twice.

Calculate the effective power transmittance:

\[
T_{\mathrm{eff},j}
=
m_{1/2,j}^{2}.
\]

Calculate the fading-amplitude variance:

\[
V_{\mathrm{fad},j}
=
m_{1,j}-T_{\mathrm{eff},j}.
\]

Verify:

\[
V_{\mathrm{fad},j}\geq0
\]

within numerical precision.

The fading-induced modulation variance contribution is:

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{fad},j}
=
V_{\mathrm{fad},j}V_A.
\]

This term must not be silently omitted.
