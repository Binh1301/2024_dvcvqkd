\section{Source moments: definitions, finite matrices, and AP}
On the source span define
\begin{align}
\tau&=\sum_i p_i|\alpha_i\rangle\langle\alpha_i|,\quad
C=\Tr(\sqrt\tau\,\hat a\sqrt\tau\,\hat a^\dagger),\\
\hat a_\tau&=\sqrt\tau\,\hat a\tau^{-1/2},\\
w&=\sum_i p_i\{\langle\alpha_i|\hat a_\tau^\dagger\hat a_\tau|\alpha_i\rangle
-|\langle\alpha_i|\hat a_\tau|\alpha_i\rangle|^2\}.
\end{align}
The Gram operations implement these definitions recorded in the security
specification. C is a source correlation kernel and w a non-Gaussian variance
penalty, depending on the complete ensemble, not distance.
\DI\ (\src{src/cvqkd/gram_moments.py}{28}):
\begin{equation}
G_{ij}=\sqrt{p_ip_j}e^{-(|\alpha_i|^2+|\alpha_j|^2)/2+\alpha_i^*\alpha_j}.
\end{equation}
With columns $A_i=\sqrt{p_i}|\alpha_i\rangle$, $G=A^\dagger A,\tau=AA^\dagger$
share positive spectra. Positive weights/distinct coherent states give a source span of dimension 256;
threshold-retained float64 rank is not mathematical rank. No Fock cutoff is used.
For 64 prototypes $(p_k,z_k)$, $p_k$ is a single-symbol probability, orbit mass $4p_k$:
\begin{align}
(B_d)_{k\ell}&=\sqrt{p_kp_\ell}e^{-(|z_k|^2+|z_\ell|^2)/2+z_k^*\mathrm i^dz_\ell},\\
G_s&=\operatorname{Herm}\left(\sum_{d=0}^3\mathrm i^{sd}B_d\right),\quad
\operatorname{Herm}(A)=(A+A^\dagger)/2.
\end{align}
These are four 64-by-64 sectors. Write $s^-=(s-1)\bmod4$,
$D_z=\operatorname{diag}(z_k)$, $D_p=\operatorname{diag}(1/(2\sqrt{p_k}))$,
$S_s=\sqrt{G_s}$. \DI\ in the fast path:
\begin{align}
B_s^{op}&=S_sD_zS_{s^-}^{-1},&A_s^{op}&=G_sD_zG_{s^-}^{-1},\\
Q_s&=S_sD_p,&T_s&=A_s^{op}Q_{s^-},\\
C&=\sum_s\operatorname{ReTr}(S_sB_s^{op}S_{s^-}(B_s^{op})^\dagger),\\
d_k&=\sum_{s,j}\overline{(Q_s)_{jk}}(T_s)_{jk},\\
w&=\sum_k4p_k\sum_{s,j}|(T_s)_{jk}-(Q_s)_{jk}d_k|^2.
\end{align}
The nonnegative residual is checked against
$\sum_s\|A_s^{op}S_{s^-}\|_F^2-\sum_k4p_k|d_k|^2$.
This is finite matrix algebra, not source-moment quadrature.
The fast gate requires all sector eigenvalues positive, max/min ratio $\le10^6$,
reconstruction and residual-identity discrepancies $\le10^{-12}$, and finite moments.
Detached eigensystems are gate-only; whole-matrix Frechet functions/solves do
the calculation. Failure invokes AP, without threshold-truncating modes.

\DI\ (\src{scripts/full_support_c4_worker.py}{64,208}):
input binary64 $p,z$ hexadecimal values are replayed at 1050, 1250, and 1450 decimal digits;

R_{s^-}X&=M_s^{\mathsf T},&B_s^{op}&=R_sX^{\mathsf T},\\

\end{align}

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}

\section{Direct-Z-minus Holevo under reverse reconciliation}
Reverse reconciliation uses Bob's variable, so leakage is $\chi_{BE}$, rather than $\chi_{AE}$.
The asymptotic covariance functional provides no finite-key, composable, or
operational fading-aggregation proof.
\DI\ (\src{src/cvqkd/holevo.py}{364}; \src{src/cvqkd/covariance.py}{72}):
\begin{align}
Z_-&=2\sqrt tC-\sqrt{2t\epsilon w},\quad a=1+V_A,\quad b=1+tV_A+t\epsilon,\\
\Gamma_{AB}&=\begin{pmatrix}a&0&c&0\\0&a&0&-c\\c&0&b&0\\0&-c&0&b\end{pmatrix},
\quad c=Z_-.
\end{align}
The order is $(q_A,p_A,q_B,p_B)$; $V_A$ is computed from the validated ensemble.
Discrete C,w determine correlation, not a substituted Gaussian-modulation Z.
With $\Delta=a^2+b^2-2c^2$, $D_\Gamma=(ab-c^2)^2$:
\begin{equation}
\lambda_{1,2}=\sqrt{(\Delta\pm\sqrt{\Delta^2-4D_\Gamma})/2},\qquad
\lambda_3=a-c^2/(b+1).
\end{equation}
The Schur complement gives conditional covariance $\lambda_3 I_2$ (\DE);
+1 is ideal heterodyne, not an electronic noise mode.
\DI:
\begin{align}
g(n)&=(n+1)\log_2(n+1)-n\log_2n,\quad g(0)=0,\\
\chi_{BE}&=g((\lambda_1-1)/2)+g((\lambda_2-1)/2)-g((\lambda_3-1)/2).
\end{align}
The adopted $S(AB)-S(A|Y)$ Gaussian covariance entropy calculation uses
invariants, roots, and three entropies.
The symmetry tolerance is $10^{-8}$, physicality tolerance is $10^{-10}$,
and relative-scale floor is $10^{-15}$.
Materially negative $w$, discriminants, or squared eigenvalues fail.
An uncertainty violation $\lambda<1-10^{-10}$, nonfinite values, or
Holevo information below $-10^{-10}$ also fails.
Tiny negative $w$ and discriminants are clamped to zero with repair records.
Clamping squared eigenvalues to zero before their square roots does not remove
the uncertainty check. Within-tolerance eigenvalues below one are raised to
one for entropy evaluation, with a record.
The zero-occupation branch avoids $0\log0$; tiny negative Holevo values
are clamped to zero and recorded.
No such guard clips K.

Diagnostic mode=zstar in the direct branch means $Z_-$, not an argmax.
The caller verifies that mode. Full-interval $Z_+$/physical intersection/
maximizer exists at holevo.py:438 but is disabled for the catalog.
The general equation reference describes that different target; the available
D55 manuscript section describes direct-$Z_-$. $Z_-$ is not asserted to be a
proven worst-case Holevo maximizer, and the direct branch does not clip
correlation to repair an unphysical covariance.

\section{Alternating optimization}
\DI\ updates approximate the intended continuous coordinate problems:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal P_1:\max_{V_A>0}K_L(\nu^{(r-1)},V_A),\qquad
\mathcal P_2:\max_{\nu>0}K_L(\nu,V_A^{(r)}).
\end{equation}
Defaults are $R_{\max}=6$, $\epsilon_{\rm opt}=10^{-8}$ bits/use, and parameter tolerance $10^{-6}$,
four refinement stages, and three boundary expansions.
The raw starts are 0.10, 0.15, 0.30, and 0.80.
Each start uses the smallest available positive $V_A$ on its slice, or a supplied
warm variance with an exact hexadecimal match.
After the first distance, the preceding winner is appended as a warm start.
The original final selection uses the highest valid terminal $K$, keeping
start order on ties; independent catalog ranking breaks ties by lower $V_A$
and then lower $\nu$.

Every one-dimensional search evaluates all compatible exact slice points.
Endpoint winners request half/double-value probes, at most 3;
a singleton decreasing on one side does not close both directions.
For an interior winner $z$ with neighbors $z_-<z<z_+$, the search checks
$\max(z-z_-,z_+-z)\le10^{-6}$ at loop entry.
Otherwise it requests $(z_-+z)/2$ and then $(z+z_+)/2$, for at most four paired stages.
No extra tolerance check occurs after stage 4.
Missing sources/failures remain explicit; no source/objective interpolation.
The outer change $\Delta K$ is the objective after P2 minus the preceding objective.
Nonfinite/decreasing/warning/numerical-failure states fail.
Absolute changes greater than $10^{-8}$ continue within six cycles.
Small change plus open boundary yields UNRESOLVED\_BOUNDARY;
incomplete coordinates/history yields STAGNATED\_IN\_EXACT\_CATALOG.
Only closed local brackets, no missing/failure/open-boundary history, and the objective
tolerance yield CONVERGED\_NUMERICALLY.
Unresolved history accumulates; immobile parameters do not erase it.
Stagnation/local closure are not global certificates; stopping tolerance is
not total numerical error.
