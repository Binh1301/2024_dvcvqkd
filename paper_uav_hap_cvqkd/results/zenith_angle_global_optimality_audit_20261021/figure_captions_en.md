# Stage-1 figure captions

All figures use only the authenticated historical 218-source experiment.
Lines connect actually evaluated samples without smoothing, extrapolation, or
claims about intermediate values. No new sources or rates were evaluated.
Uniform and MB pilot-refinement series are absent because the exploratory
domain has not been approved. PDF vector and PNG 350 dpi versions are supplied.

- **Fig. 0.** Historical MB source-catalog coverage in raw shaping parameter
  and modulation variance. Diamonds identify catalog-selected sites, annotated
  by their selected zenith angles; the dashed line marks the numerical VA cap.
  The logarithmic variance axis resolves the catalog's broad range.
- **Fig. A.** Historical finite-catalog optimum of Uniform 256-QAM versus
  zenith angle, including signed negative rates. New refinement is not executed.
- **Fig. B.** Fixed MB and historical finite-catalog MB optimum versus zenith
  angle. All 31 angles are retained; no refined series is inferred.
- **Fig. U.** Historical Uniform raw-rate samples versus modulation variance
  at six representative angles. Diamonds mark finite-catalog winners; squares
  mark interior sampled-neighborhood maxima, without continuous certification.
  A logarithmic VA axis resolves the small-variance region and high-VA tail.
- **Fig. 1.** Historical MB raw-rate slices versus variance at fixed raw
  shaping parameters. Every marker is an actual saved exact-source evaluation.
- **Fig. 2.** Historical MB raw-rate slices versus raw shaping parameter for
  several fixed variances. Irregular sampling is retained.
- **Fig. 3.** Raw rates at the 182 historically evaluated MB parameter sites
  at 48 degrees. Colors encode saved rates; no interpolated contour or heatmap
  is constructed. The outlined diamond marks the finite-catalog maximum.
- **Fig. D.** Historical source parameters selected versus zenith angle.
  Open red squares identify selections at the VA catalog cap; separate panels
  show MB variance, raw nu, and Uniform variance.

**Fig. C (improvement percentages)** and **original-versus-refined optimum
markers** are NOT_RUN: there is no new exact rate from which an improvement
can be calculated. A zero-gain curve would incorrectly imply refinement was
performed and is therefore not drawn. `optimality_improvement.csv` records N/A.

All results concern the unchanged mixed 2D-I/Q MI and homodyne-conditioned
Holevo functional. They do not establish physically secure key generation.
