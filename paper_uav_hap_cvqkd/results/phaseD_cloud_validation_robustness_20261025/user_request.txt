## 3. Task A — Mathematical Validation of the New Cloud Model

The new cloud model is:

\[
\mathbf G_n\mid\boldsymbol\mu_n
\sim\mathcal N(\boldsymbol\mu_n,\boldsymbol\Gamma),
\]

where:

\[
\mu_{j,n}\overset{\rm iid}{\sim}\mathcal U(-1,1),
\]

and:

\[
\Gamma_{ij}=9(0.9)^{|i-j|}.
\]

The mean vector and Gaussian innovations are independently regenerated at each time slot.

No temporal Gaussian AR(1) is used.

### A1. Conditional covariance

Verify that:

\[
\operatorname{Cov}
(\mathbf G_n\mid\boldsymbol\mu_n)
=\boldsymbol\Gamma.
\]

Test the covariance using samples with a fixed mean vector, or subtract the sampled mean vector from each realization.

Check:

- Diagonal variance = 9
- Off-diagonal covariance = \(9(0.9)^{|i-j|}\)
- Symmetry
- Positive definiteness
- Numerical Cholesky reconstruction accuracy

### A2. Unconditional covariance

Because the Gaussian mean vector is independently sampled using Uniform(-1,1):

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\mu_{j,n})=\frac13.
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf G_n)
=
\boldsymbol\Gamma+\frac13\mathbf I.
}
\]

Explicitly derive and numerically verify this expression.

In particular:

\[
\operatorname{Var}(G_{j,n})
=
9+\frac13.
\]

For i ≠ j:

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(G_{i,n},G_{j,n})
=
9(0.9)^{|i-j|}.
\]

Distinguish conditional covariance from unconditional covariance in all reports.

Do not report Gamma as the unconditional covariance when mu is independently randomized.

### A3. Temporal independence

Verify that the new cloud generator does not reuse previous Gaussian fields or share a persistent random mean across slots.

Measure empirical temporal correlations at several lags.

Under the documented independent-slot model, the theoretical temporal covariance is zero for nonzero slot lags.

Use confidence intervals or Monte Carlo uncertainty when interpreting empirical correlations.

Do not mistake a small nonzero empirical correlation for proof of a temporal process.

### A4. Binary threshold validation

The baseline uses:

\[
c_{j,n}=\mathbb I\{G_{j,n}>0\}.
\]

Verify the theoretical marginal blockage probability:

\[
P(c_{j,n}=1)=0.5
\]

under the symmetric random-mean model.

For experiments with a prescribed p, inspect and independently verify the implemented threshold-calibration procedure.

The calibrated threshold must satisfy:

\[
\mathbb E_{\mu\sim U(-1,1)}
\left[
1-\Phi\left(\frac{t_p-\mu}{3}\right)
\right]=p.
\]

Compare predicted and empirical blockage probabilities for:

\[
p=0.1,0.2,\ldots,0.8.
\]

Report calibration error and sampling uncertainty.

Do not claim the threshold-calibration extension appears verbatim in Gong et al. or Sanchez Net et al. without direct evidence.

### A5. Binary spatial correlation

Compare it against the latent Gaussian correlation.
