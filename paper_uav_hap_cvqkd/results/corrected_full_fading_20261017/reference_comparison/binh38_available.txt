          IV. N UMERICAL R ESULTS & D ISCUSSIONS
    Unless otherwise noted, default parameters used in this
paper are as follows. At the HAP (Alice): HAP altitude
hHAP = 20 km, zenith angle ξ = 30◦ , divergence full-angle
θ = 233 µrad, F0 = ∞, optical wavelength λ = 1550 nm,
jitter angle θjt = 15.61 µrad. At the UAV (Bob): UAV altitude
hUAV = 1 km, receiver aperture diameter Dr = 8 cm, boresight
offsets ρx = ρy = 0 m, SD of UAV position σh = 1 m, SD of
UAV’s angular fluctuation σo = 1.5 mrad, the detector’s FOV
half-angle θFoV = 6.25 mrad. Regarding the quantum channel:
atmospheric altitude hatm = 20 km, atmospheric transmittance
τzen = 0.92, ground turbulence level Cn2 (0) = 10−14 m−2/3 ,
rms wind speed vwind = 21 m/s.
    key reconciliation efficiency β = 0.95
