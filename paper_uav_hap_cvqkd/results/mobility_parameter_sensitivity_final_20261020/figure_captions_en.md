# Final IEEE-style figure captions

**Figure 1.** Mean mobility-penalized objective versus the number of candidate UAV positions for optimized Stationary (P2), Greedy, and DP (P3), at λref = 9.468026677248516×10⁻⁶ bit/use. Deterministic nested subsets preserve the original coordinates, clear-sky P1 source rates, cloud missions, and 300-m transition constraint. Bands are pointwise 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals over the same 100 paired missions. The J = 10 graph is disconnected; the tested subsets are connected from J = 20 onward.

**Figure 2.** Impact of cloud blockage probability on (a) mean usable SKR and (b) QKD unavailability for the same three positioning schemes at λref. Spatial length and latent temporal coefficient are fixed at 250 m and 0.9. All eight evaluated probabilities, p = 0.1, 0.2, …, 0.8, use matched mission IDs and common innovations. Unavailability is the percentage of selected slots with zero usable SKR, with genuine zeros retained on linear axes. Bands reuse saved pointwise 95% paired whole-mission bootstrap intervals; the optimized objective is distinct from both SKR and availability.

**Figure 3.** Impact of latent temporal cloud correlation on (a) mean mobility-penalized objective and (b) mean total travel distance at p = 0.4, spatial length 250 m, and λref. The five saved temporal configurations share initial innovations and 100 mission IDs. Annotations identify only the DP distance endpoints. P2 and DP use the full cloud-state horizon, whereas Greedy observes the current slot. The latent coefficient ρ is distinct from binary blockage correlation. Bands are pointwise 95% whole-mission bootstrap intervals.

**Figure 4.** Evaluated usable-SKR–mobility tradeoff for Greedy and DP, with optimized Stationary as a reference point, under the default cloud configuration. Each curve connects saved policies in the original order of ten increasing penalties, rather than sorting by distance. DP annotations identify zero and representative penalties. Horizontal and vertical error bars are pointwise 95% confidence intervals for the two means, reconstructed from saved missions using existing bootstrap counts; they are not joint confidence regions. Coincident high-penalty DP points and the stationary reference are retained in the source data. These evaluated policies are not claimed to be globally optimal physical Pareto frontiers.

All figures use 100 paired missions, a common 7.2-inch width, vector PDF and PNG at 350 dpi. Stationary is blue/solid/circle, Greedy orange/dashed/diamond, and DP red/solid/triangle. No smoothing, interpolation, cloud generation, or trajectory optimization is performed. Figure numbering is local to this numerical-results package and does not overwrite the figures or placeholders in the external manuscript.

The multi-curve parameter-sensitivity presentation follows the experimental presentation style of S. Gong et al., “Network Availability Maximization for Free-Space Optical Satellite Communications,” IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, vol. 9, no. 3, pp. 411–415, 2020, [doi:10.1109/LWC.2019.2958804](https://doi.org/10.1109/LWC.2019.2958804). Its numerical results and ground-station selection model are not transferred to this UAV experiment.
