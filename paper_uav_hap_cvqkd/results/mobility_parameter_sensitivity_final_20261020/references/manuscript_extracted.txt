Abstract—This paper investigates mobility-aware unmanned
aerial vehicle (UA V) positioning in a high-altitude-platform
(HAP)-to-UA V free-space optical continuous-variable quantum
key distribution (CV-QKD) system under cloud blockage. Prob-
abilistic constellation shaping is optimized over a finite source
catalog to maximize the clear-sky secret key rate (SKR) at each
candidate UA V position. We first identify the best stationary
position and then formulate a mobility-aware trajectory opti-
mization problem that balances the average usable SKR against
a normalized flight-distance penalty subject to a per-slot speed
constraint. The resulting finite-horizon discrete problem is solved
exactly by dynamic programming on the candidate-position grid.
The evaluation compares source shaping, stationary positioning,
and cloud-aware UA V mobility, using greedy positioning as a
low-complexity trajectory baseline.
Index Terms—Continuous-variable quantum key distribution
(CV–QKD), unmanned aerial vehicle (UA V), free-space optics
(FSO), high-altitude platform (HAP), probabilistic constellation
shaping (PCS), dynamic programming (DP).

IV. CONCLUSION
This work formulates a finite-catalog source optimization
and mobility-aware positioning framework for cloud-affected
HAP–UA V CV-QKD. Source parameters and stationary po-
sitioning are selected by finite search, while the discrete
trajectory is optimized exactly by dynamic programming for a
known cloud-state horizon. The proposed evaluation separates
shaping gain from mobility gain and explicitly accounts for the
tradeoff between usable SKR and UA V travel distance. Online
decision-making with uncertain future cloud states remains an
extension beyond the present model.
