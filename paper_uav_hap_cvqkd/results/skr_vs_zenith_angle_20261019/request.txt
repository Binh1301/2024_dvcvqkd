## 5. SKR Calculation — Preserve the Manuscript Implementation

Calculate

\[
\boxed{
K=\beta I_{AB}^{2D}-\chi_{BE}.
}
\]

### Mutual Information

Use the manuscript-style normalized 2D Gaussian implementation:

\[
I_{AB}^{2D}
=
\sum_{k=1}^{256}p_k
\int_{\mathbb C}
p(y|\alpha_k)
\log_2
\frac{p(y|\alpha_k)}
{\sum_jp_jp(y|\alpha_j)}
\,d^2y.
\]

With

\[
p(y|\alpha_k)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi\sigma_\zeta^2}
\exp\left[
-\frac{|y-2\sqrt{\eta_f}\alpha_k|^2}
{2\sigma_\zeta^2}
\right],
\]

and

\[
\sigma_\zeta^2=1+\eta_f\xi_f.
\]

Mandatory:

- Use both real and imaginary components.
- Do not divide MI by two.
- Do not switch to 1D homodyne MI.
- Do not introduce heterodyne noise corrections.
- Use Gauss–Hermite quadrature with convergence checks.
- Preserve the source normalization used in the verified exact-source cache, \(V_A=2\langle n\rangle\).

### Holevo Information

Keep the manuscript equations:

\[
V=V_A+1,
\]

\[
W=1+\eta_fV_A+\eta_f\xi_f,
\]

\[
Z=
2\sqrt{\eta_f}C

\[
