Figure A — Dynamic Gaussian Cloud Map and Binary Cloud Blockage Model. A synthetic CLWC field, generated by a reproducible sum of 400 Gaussian components and clipped to 0–15 mg/m³, is sampled over a 16 km × 2 km domain at 40 m spacing. The lower panel applies the illustrative threshold L_th = 8 mg/m³ to the same samples. Red windows represent 2 km × 2 km local operating regions with 50 × 50 cloud samples. Physical cloud motion is 5 m/s to the left; the equivalent reference-map observation windows shift to the right by 5t. The 300 s window is offset by half a background cell and its local samples are obtained using bilinear interpolation. The threshold is a model parameter, not a measured universal opacity threshold. This horizontal cloud-property map alone does not determine whether a three-dimensional HAP–UAV optical path intersects a cloud layer. Under the assumed complete-blockage model, such an intersection implies zero effective transmittance and zero secret key rate; non-blocking regions do not imply unit transmittance. UAV positions are continuous.

Figure B — Threshold sensitivity for the same sampled field. Black indicates cells with CLWC at or above the indicated threshold. Percentages are computed over the full 400 × 50 map. The red outline shows the same t = 0 local window in each panel.
