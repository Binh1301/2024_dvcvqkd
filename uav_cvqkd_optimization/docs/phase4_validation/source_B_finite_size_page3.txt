III. SECURITY ANALYSIS AND KEY RATE CALCULATION
This section presents a comprehensive security analysis for
the CV-QKD protocol operating over turbulent ground-to-UAV
FSO channels. The achievable SKR is derived under three
scenarios: (A) the asymptotic scenario assuming an infinite
number of exchanged quantum states, (B) the finite-size sce-
nario accounting for statistical fluctuations due to limited data
blocks, and (C) the finite-size scenario enhanced with AO,
which mitigates turbulence-induced phase distortions. In all
cases, the modulation variance VA is numerically optimized
as a function of transmission distance to maximize the SKR.
