I. PROBLEM FORMULATION
A. Problem Descriptions
We consider a HAP-assisted UAV-based CV-QKD sys-
tem operating under dynamic cloud conditions. The system
consists of a quasi-stationary HAP and a rotary-wing UAV
connected through an FSO link. The UAV operates at a
fixed altitude within a 2 km × 2 km area A, with continuous
horizontal coordinates qk = (xk, yk) ∈A.
The cloud field is assumed to move from right to left at a
constant velocity of 5 m/s. With a slot duration of ∆t = 1 s,
the cloud distribution shifts by 5 m per time slot. The CLWC
values are updated accordingly, while the UAV operating area
remains fixed.
The operation period is divided into K time slots of duration
∆t = 1 s. Unlike the slot duration, the number of slots K is
variable and limited by the UAV’s available energy Emax. Let
q0 denote the initial UAV position. The displacement in slot
k is
dk = ∥qk −qk−1∥.
(1)
Moving clouds may interrupt the HAP–UAV optical link,
resulting in zero secret key generation during blocked periods.
Meanwhile, UAV movement and hovering consume propulsion
energy. Our objective is to optimize the UAV trajectory and
operational duration to maximize secret key generation under
the available energy budget.
