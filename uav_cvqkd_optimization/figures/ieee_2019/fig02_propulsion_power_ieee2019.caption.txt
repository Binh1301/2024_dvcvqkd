Figure 02. Rotary-Wing UAV Propulsion Power Versus Flight Speed. Total, blade-profile, induced and parasite powers are computed from Eq.(12), with coefficients derived from Eq.(64) using the rounded Table I aircraft parameters of Zeng, Xu and Zhang, IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 18(4), 2329-2345, April 2019, doi:10.1109/TWC.2019.2902559. The speed limit is 30 m/s from Section V. The induced velocity is derived as 4.028542625 m/s; Table I reports its rounded value 4.03 m/s. ME minimizes P(V); MR minimizes P(V)/V over positive speed. Both are found by grid-bracketed bounded numerical refinement and checked against a dense sweep. This is a reference-aircraft model illustration, not a measurement or a CV-QKD mission result.
